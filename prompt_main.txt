If 'e' is declared, the AI must label the output line as "Status: Executed" and analyze the environment change in the $snippet. */
ss($status, $snippet) {

  --> Justify the decision by explaining why this structure is robust. --> end1
  
  1 -- no --> 2 Is the value of $status equal to 'e'?
